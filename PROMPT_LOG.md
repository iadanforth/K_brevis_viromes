**Done:** Answered no, and separated the two conflated jobs: geNomad does not predict genes
(it calls pyrodigal-gv internally, as this pipeline does explicitly); its main purpose is
virus *identification* (`end-to-end`), which this pipeline does not use, only `annotate`.

limitation from the paper's own Supplemental Data 4: geNomad annotated 595 of 5,912 genes
across their 11 GVMAGs (10.1%, range 6.1-14.4%). Recommended keeping geNomad (matches the
paper, good for hallmark genes) and adding eggNOG-mapper or the GVOG HMMs already shipped
with GVClass if functional annotation is wanted later. Added that caveat, with the numbers,
