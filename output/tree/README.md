## How to use these in iTOL

1. Upload `kbrevis_gvmags.treefile` at https://itol.embl.de/upload.cgi
2. **Midpoint-root it** (Advanced → Midpoint root). The tree is unrooted, and branching
   order means nothing until you do. Booker et al. midpoint-rooted theirs.
3. Drag these annotation files onto the rendered tree:

| file | what it does |
|---|---|
| `itol_tree_colors.txt` | your 17 vOTUs' branches and labels in red and bold — otherwise unfindable among 1,564 tips |
| `itol_order_colorstrip.txt` | colour strip by viral order, colour-blind-safe, with legend |
| `itol_family_colorstrip.txt` | the same by family (10 commonest, 1,361 of 1,564 tips) |
| `itol_labels.txt` | renames our tips to `vOTU01 \| SRR28004148`, with `[N genomes]` for multi-member vOTUs |
| `itol_popup.txt` | hover text: completeness, length, nearest GVMAGs V2 relative with ANI/AF, ecosystem |

Suggested first view: circular, labels off, order colour strip + `itol_tree_colors.txt`.

To display certainty instead of UFBoot, upload `kbrevis_gvmags.IC_branch_labels.nwk`
(RAxML IC scores as branch labels) as a separate tree.

## Where your vOTUs landed

Full table in `votu_tree_context.tsv`, generated by `scripts/votu_tree_context.py`.
Every placement is supported at ≥97 UFBoot, all but vOTU01 at 100.

Eleven vOTUs sit with *Imitervirales* references (*Mimiviridae*, *Mesomimiviridae*,
f_IM_14, f_IM_07); vOTU14 with *Pimascovirales*, vOTU15 with *Algavirales*.

**Two things to look at:**

1. **vOTU04 is placed with *Asfuvirales*** (f_AF_01) at 100 support, but step 08's GVClass
   called it *Imitervirales* f_IM_07. That call was a 4-vs-2 marker vote
   (`Imitervirales:4(66.67%),Asfuvirales:2(33.33%)`), so GVClass was never confident. The
   concatenated seven-marker tree uses the markers jointly rather than voting, and it has no
   GVMAGs V2 relative for ANI to break the tie — so prefer the tree. Any per-vOTU taxonomy
   table needs a footnote here.
2. **Two clades contain only your vOTUs, with no reference interleaved:**
   {vOTU03, vOTU09, vOTU13} and {vOTU07, vOTU12}. These are candidate *K. brevis*-associated
   lineages absent from GVMAGs V2. **Caveat:** vOTU13 has 74.8% missing data (~1,022 real
   amino acids), and data-poor taxa attach to each other with spuriously high support, so
   its membership is not established. {vOTU03, vOTU09} (0.0% and 6.7% missing) and
   {vOTU07, vOTU12} (both 0.0%) are credible.

**Best follow-up:** re-run step 10 with `--tier skani` (6,266 references instead of 1,523)
to test whether a reference falls inside those clades and dissolves them.

## Not in the tree

**vOTU05 and vOTU19 are absent entirely.** They carried none of the seven marker genes, so
`ncldv_markersearch` dropped them. vOTU05 is the open question: CheckV called it 100%
complete at 203 kb with no GVMAGs V2 relative, yet it has no core *Nucleocytoviricota*
markers and no resolved order. Do not report it as a giant virus genome without more
evidence.
